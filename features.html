Review Collection

8 features
01

Review Sites

Connect every major review platform — Google, Facebook, Yelp, TripAdvisor and more — in one place. VeLora pulls in new reviews automatically so nothing slips past you. Centralized monitoring saves hours every week.

02

VeLora Link

One review link your customers can open from SMS, email, or a QR code on the counter. The flow is tuned to route happy customers straight to a five-star review. Built to convert without feeling pushy.

03

Sentiment Filter

Route positive and negative experiences differently. Delighted customers head to public review sites, while unhappy ones land in private feedback first. Fewer public one-stars, faster fixes.

04

Thank You Page

After leaving a review, customers land on a customizable thank-you page. Use it for a promo, a booking link, or a next step. It keeps the moment of goodwill working for you.

05

Private Feedback

Critical feedback goes to a private inbox instead of a public profile. You resolve the issue directly, before it becomes a review. This alone changes your public rating.

06

Video Testimonials

Collect video reviews through a simple link or QR code. Clips are stored in your dashboard, ready for your site or socials. Nothing builds trust faster than a real face.

07

AI Review Suggestions

Help customers write a better review with AI-drafted suggestions. They rate the visit, add a note, and VeLora generates natural review text to paste on Google. Less blank-page hesitation, more finished reviews.

08

Custom Review Sites

Add any platform beyond the standard set. Niche industry directories or regional listings can join the same review flow. Your customers only ever see one link.

Outreach & Campaigns

8 features
01

Custom Prompt

Tune how VeLora's AI writes on your behalf — the tone, the length, and the details it should never leave out. Every reply, request, and follow-up still sounds like your business, not a generic bot.

02

Review Aggregator

Every review from every connected platform lands in one unified feed. Filter by location, rating, or source, and never miss a mention because it was buried on a site you don't check often.

03

Review Replies

Respond to Google and Yelp reviews — and every other platform — without opening a dozen tabs. AI drafts a reply matched to the review's tone; you approve it, or let it post automatically.

04

Automated Campaigns

Build multi-step review request sequences that follow up automatically. If a customer doesn't respond to the first text, VeLora tries email next, then stops — so nobody gets chased forever.

05

Smart Send Timing

VeLora learns when each customer is most likely to respond and times the request accordingly. Fewer messages sent into voicemail or a full inbox at midnight.

06

Multi-Location Requests

Run request campaigns across every location from one dashboard. Each location's reviews route to the right team, but reporting rolls up to a single view.

07

Segmented Lists

Group contacts by visit type, spend, or service so campaigns speak to the right audience. A first-time customer and a five-year regular don't get the same message.

08

Follow-Up Reminders

Automatically nudge customers who opened a request but didn't finish it. One well-timed reminder recovers a meaningful share of abandoned reviews.
