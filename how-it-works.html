01 · Setup · Once ~10 minutes

Program the card. Never touch it again.

Your Designer Enterprise Card arrives ready to pair. Tap it to your own phone once to link it to your account, connect your Google Business Profile, and choose exactly where every future tap should lead.

Step 1

Pair your card

One tap on your own phone links the chip to your Designer Enterprise account — no app, no serial number to type in.

Step 2

Connect your platforms

Google, Yelp, Facebook, and 17+ other listing sites get claimed through a single one-click login.

Choose the destination

Pick what a tap opens — review prompt, booking calendar, or contact card — and change it anytime from your dashboard.

02 · Tap · Every visit ~3 seconds a tap

Hand it over. The rest happens in their pocket.

Set the card by the register, clip it to a lanyard, or hand it across the table at the end of a job. One tap wakes the customer's own phone and opens your review page before they've even sat back down.

Step 1

Card meets phone

The NFC chip fires a native prompt on iOS and Android alike; a printed QR code on the card covers anyone with NFC switched off.

Step 2

Page opens instantly

Your branded Designer Enterprise Link loads in under a second, already carrying your logo, colors, and name.

Step 3

One tap to rate

No login, no typing, no app download — just a star tap on a screen they already trust.

03 · Filter · Every rating Automatic

The Sentiment Filter reads the room first.

A five-star tap and a two-star tap shouldn't travel the same road. Designer Enterprise reads the rating before anything goes public, so happy customers boost your listings and frustrated ones reach you privately instead.

Step 1

4–5★ heads public

Routed straight to whichever platform — Google, Yelp, Facebook — needs the boost most that week.

Step 2

1–3★ stays private

A short "tell us what happened" form lands in your inbox, not on the internet.

Step 3

You get the first word

Reach out and make it right before a rough visit ever becomes a public review.

04 · Reply · Every day ~2 minutes a day

AI drafts the reply. You just tap approve.

Every new review — glowing or gutting — lands in one inbox with a reply already written in your voice. Skim it, edit a line if you want, and publish. Five-star thank-yous can even post themselves.

Step 1

Step 2

One tap to publish, or rewrite it in seconds — your call, every time.

Step 3

It posts everywhere

Straight to Google, Yelp, or Facebook, logged in one timeline with your response time attached.
